## Policy

A string is **structured coverage** only when the sentence is honest (no `unparsed` marker) **and** carries no `should_be_structured` string. `fragment` is the only legal free text and must sit on an unparsed marker. Closed vocabularies (types, zones, verbs, keywords, player/card references, mana strings, quantifiers) may be multi-word; values in the allow-lists (trigger heads Whenever/When/As/If/At, single colours) are benign.

## Calibration (human review v3)

The human labels found false negatives: compound atoms, prose modifiers, mana-choice amounts, raw costs and anaphoric possessives. The stricter rules are field-aware additions to the detector, not path reclassifications.

`compound_atom` flags atomic fields (`verb`, `value`, `kind`, `stat`, `phase`, `which`, `player`) containing ` or `/` and `/a comma, e.g. `trigger.event.verb = "enters or attacks"` and `card_type.value = "instant or sorcery"`. `modifier_prose` keeps prose `modifier` values (with/greater/less) flagged even when a subfield was closed. `amount_mana` catches amounts containing a mana symbol. `raw_cost` is implemented but fired 0 times on this snapshot (no raw cost strings remain). `anaphora` flags `its/their/his/her` in a structured slot, excluding the dedicated `possessive` field.
