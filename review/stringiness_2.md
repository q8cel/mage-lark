## Policy

A string is **structured coverage** only when the sentence is honest (no `unparsed` marker) **and** carries no `should_be_structured` string. `fragment` is the only legal free text and must sit on an unparsed marker. Closed vocabularies (types, zones, verbs, keywords, player/card references, mana strings, quantifiers) may be multi-word; values in the allow-lists (trigger heads Whenever/When/As/If/At, single colours) are benign.

### String moved to string

- The new structured objects are vocabulary, not prose: `duration.kind/event/object`, `amount.kind/unit/of`, `counter.kind/count`, `to.zone/owner`, `trigger.when.phase/position/player`, `pt` components. The field-aware policy classifies them closed by suffix and they stop being flagged.
- The closed-vocabulary outlier guard (new) flags >4-token prose inside a closed field: `effect.who` 'any number of target players', `effect.owner` 'The owner of target artifact or creature...', `trigger.event.verb` 'attacks for the first time each turn', counter-kind prose, shared suffixes (10 sites).
- Still prose after the cutover: `keywords.modifier` is a stringified dict (`for {'card_type': ...}`, 29 occ), `trigger.event.object.label` / `effect.card.label` are noun phrases (68 occ), and the unchanged families (`predicate` 222, `quoted-ability` 325, `target` 58, `target-label` 246) are genuinely unfixed.
- One legitimate false positive was fixed: `*.modifier.which` ('the chosen', 'that') is a closed qualifier; `which` was added to the closed vocabulary. The policy was regenerated from the post-fix snapshot (1,371 paths: 1,173 closed, 178 structured, 20 free text); new `stat/kind/zone/op/phase` subfields are closed by suffix, not false positives.
- `phrase` grew +24 because the cutover exposed new `of`/`relative` subfields that are still prose ('that was dealt damage this turn', 'mana spent to cast this spell'); that is a real remaining family, not a replacement artefact.
